Privacy Policy

Roz Hisaab · Singhani Wealth Management · Last updated 28 October 2026

1. Who we are

Roz Hisaab is an app for keeping household accounts: daily services, spending and bills, documents, rent, tuition and trusted helpers. The app is operated by Singhani Wealth Management (Udyam UDYAM-HR-02-0041040), Village Singhani, Loharu, District Bhiwani, Haryana 127201 ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and your choices, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

2. Data that stays on your phone

Everything you enter (services, spending, bills, documents, tenants, students, helpers and your profile) is stored on your phone. We cannot see it. It is not uploaded unless you sign in and turn on Cloud sync and family sharing.

3. Photos, PIN and fingerprint

Document photos you add stay on your phone. They are not uploaded, backed up or synced. If you turn on App lock, your PIN is saved on your phone only as a scrambled code (a hash), never as the PIN itself. Fingerprint and face unlock are handled by your phone. Roz Hisaab never sees or stores your fingerprint or face.

4. Kaarigars near you

To show kaarigars near you, the app sends your pincode, or your approximate location if you tap Use my location, to our server for that search only. It is not stored or linked to you. Kaarigars who list themselves on our website agree that their name, work, area and phone number are shown to app users near them. They can ask us to remove their listing at any time.

5. Invite code account

When the app is online for the first time, it creates an account on our server without any personal details. It holds only your invite code and any referral rewards. Your hisaab stays on your phone. If you later sign in, the same account is used.

6. Signing in

You can sign in with a code sent to your email, or with Continue with Google. With Google, we receive only your name, email address and Google account ID from Google; never your password, contacts or files. Signing in is needed only for Cloud sync and family sharing.

7. Data we process if you turn on Cloud sync

If you sign in, we store your email address, name, mobile number, city and app language, and copies of the records you enter, so they can be backed up and shared with family members you invite. This can include names and phone numbers of people you record, such as tenants, students, parents and helpers. Only add people's details if you have a genuine reason and, where needed, their permission.

8. Family sharing

In a shared family hisaab, every member can see the records in it, the names of the members and their role, who added, changed or deleted each entry, who paid for each expense and who marked a bill, rent or fee as paid. These names also appear in reports a member downloads. The owner, co-owners and admins approve who joins and can remove members. When someone leaves, the entries they made stay with their name. When someone deletes their account, their name on shared entries is replaced with "Former member", and the family passes to another member.

9. Family money

The owner or a co-owner can record money given to a member, such as pocket money, and members can record money they return. This is only a record that the family can see; no money is held or moved by Roz Hisaab.

10. History and deleted entries

So families can see who changed what, we keep a history of changes to shared entries for 1 year. Deleted entries can be brought back for 30 days, and are then removed from our servers.

11. Purposes

We use this data only to sync and back up your records, share them with the family members you choose, send the reminders and alerts you see, provide Pro features, apply referral rewards, answer support requests and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

12. Payments

Rent, fees and bills are paid from your own UPI app directly to the other person's bank account. Roz Hisaab never receives or holds this money and never sees your UPI PIN or bank details. Pro subscriptions are bought through Google Play (or the App Store on iPhone); they process that payment and share only the purchase status with us.

13. Service providers

Cloud data is hosted by Supabase on servers in India (Mumbai). Sign-in with Google is handled by Google. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play and the App Store. If you connect Google Drive, backups are saved in a private app folder in your own Google Drive that only Roz Hisaab can open; we do not receive them. You can disconnect anytime in Profile. These providers act on our instructions and protect your data.

14. Sponsored cards

Free users may see sponsored cards from advertisers. We count how many times a card is shown and tapped, without linking this to your records or identity. Pro users do not see sponsored cards.

15. Notifications and permissions

With your permission, the app shows a short note every day at the time you choose (festival wishes, bills and renewals due, tips), renewal alerts, rent, fee and bill due alerts, and family alerts such as join requests, delete requests and big spending. These are made on your phone from your own hisaab and are not sent through our servers. You can change the time or turn them off in Profile. The app also keeps reminders after the phone restarts, uses your approximate location only when you tap Use my location, and uses fingerprint or face only for App lock. It opens your dialer, WhatsApp and UPI apps only when you tap a button. It does not read your contacts, messages or photos.

16. Our team's access

A small number of authorised team members use a protected admin panel to give support and run the service. They can see an account's email, name, phone, city, language, plan and subscription dates, referral details, and how many entries a family has, but not the entries themselves. They also verify or hide kaarigar listings and manage sponsored cards. Every action in the admin panel is logged. We open a family's records only if you ask us for help and agree to it.

17. How long we keep data

Cloud data is kept while your account is active. The history of changes is kept for 1 year and deleted entries for 30 days. When you delete your cloud account, your account and the cloud records of a family you use alone are deleted within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something longer. Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.

18. Deleting your account

In the app: Profile > Family sharing > Delete cloud account. Without the app: email support@rozhisaab.in from the email you signed in with. Details: https://rozhisaab.in/delete-account.html

19. Your rights

You can see and correct your data in the app at any time, copy all of it from Profile, and delete your cloud account. You can also ask us about your data, ask for correction or erasure, withdraw consent, nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance, using the contacts below.

20. Children

Roz Hisaab is meant for adults. If you are under 18, use the app only with a parent or guardian, who must agree to this policy on your behalf.

21. Security

Data sent to our servers is encrypted in transit, each family's records can be read only by its members, and admin panel actions are checked and logged on the server. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone locked and your email account safe.

22. Grievance officer and contact

23. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date below and tell you in the app. Last updated 28 October 2026.
