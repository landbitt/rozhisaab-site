Delete your Roz Hisaab account

Roz Hisaab · Singhani Wealth Management · Last updated 28 October 2026

Your hisaab is kept on your phone. We only hold data on our server if you signed in for Cloud sync or family sharing. This page explains how to delete that account and its data.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Roz Hisaab and tap your initials at the top right to open My profile.
  2. Tap Family sharing (on some versions: Backup and sync).
  3. Tap Delete cloud account, then tap Tap again to delete to confirm.

You are signed out at once and your cloud account is removed.

Option 2: by email, without the app

If you no longer have the app or the phone, email support@rozhisaab.in from the same email address you signed in with (or the Gmail you used with Continue with Google). Subject: Delete my Roz Hisaab account. We may reply to that address to confirm it is you, and we tell you when it is done. You can also reach us on WhatsApp +91 96257 46088, but the request must be confirmed from the signed-in email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name, mobile number, city, app language and Google account ID.
  • Your invite code and referral rewards, and your subscription record on our server.
  • Any family hisaab where you are the only member, with all its entries, history and deleted items.
  • Your requests to join a family.

What is kept, and why

  • Shared family entries stay with the family. Entries you added to a family that others also use are part of their household accounts, so they are not removed. Your name on them, and in their edit history, is replaced with "Former member".
  • If you owned a shared family, it passes to the next person (a co-owner first, then an admin, then the member who joined first), so the family does not lose its hisaab.
  • Payments for Pro are handled by Google Play or the App Store. Their own records of the purchase are kept by them under their policies. Cancel a subscription in the Play Store or App Store; deleting your account does not cancel it.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep, only for as long as required.

How long it takes

Deleting in the app takes effect immediately. Email requests are completed within 30 days, and usually within 7 days. Server backups that may still hold a copy are overwritten within 30 days.

What we do not touch

  • Data on your phone. Your hisaab stays in the app until you clear it (My profile > App lock, privacy and data > Delete all my data) or uninstall the app.
  • Your Google Drive backups. They are in your own Google Drive, which we cannot open. Delete them from Google Drive on a computer: Settings > Manage apps > Roz Hisaab > Options > Delete hidden app data.

Other ways to control your data

You can sign out, leave a family, or remove single entries at any time without deleting your account. Deleted entries can be restored for 30 days and are then removed. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
